# 📋 Questionnaire & Self Assessment Complete Guide

*The Return of Attention - PAHM Meditation App*

---

## **OVERVIEW**

This guide provides the complete questions and answers for the two-part assessment system that forms the foundation of the PAHM (Present Attention and Happiness Matrix) meditation app. The assessment consists of:

1. **Questionnaire**: 27 comprehensive questions collected **only once** after account creation
2. **Self Assessment**: 6-category attachment evaluation with **3 different types** at progression milestones

**Assessment Timeline:**
- **Account Creation**: Questionnaire (one-time) + Initial Self-Assessment
- **After Stage 3**: Mid Self-Assessment (progress tracking)
- **After Stage 6**: Final Self-Assessment (transformation measurement)

# **PART 2: SELF ASSESSMENT (6 Categories)**
*Progressive assessment system with 3 types at different milestones*

## **ASSESSMENT TYPES & TIMING**

### **1. Initial Self-Assessment**
- **When**: Immediately after account creation (after questionnaire completion)
- **Purpose**: Baseline attachment measurement for personalized guidance
- **Requirement**: Mandatory before accessing any meditation stages

### **2. Mid Self-Assessment** 
- **When**: After completing first 3 stages (Seeker, PAHM Trainee, PAHM Beginner)
- **Purpose**: Track spiritual progress and attachment reduction
- **Requirement**: Required to unlock advanced stages (4-6)

### **3. Final Self-Assessment**
- **When**: After completing all 6 stages (full PAHM program)
- **Purpose**: Measure transformation and spiritual growth achieved
- **Requirement**: Optional but recommended for completion tracking

## **SCORING SYSTEM**

### **3-Choice Scale Options:**
- **"I don't have particular preferences for this"** = **+12 points** (non-attachment bonus)
  - *Description*: "I'm generally content with whatever comes my way in this area"
- **"I have some preferences, but I'm flexible"** = **-7 points** (mild attachment penalty)
  - *Description*: "I enjoy certain things more than others, but I adapt easily"
- **"I have strong preferences and specific likes/dislikes"** = **-15 points** (strong attachment penalty)
  - *Description*: "There are clear things I love or avoid in this area"

### **Total Score Calculation:**
- **Range**: -90 to +72 points
- **Interpretation**: Higher scores indicate less attachment (more spiritual progress)
