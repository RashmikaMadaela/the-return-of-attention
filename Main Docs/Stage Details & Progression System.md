# The Return of Attention - Stage Details & Progression System

## 📋 Overview
This document details the complete stage progression system for "The Return of Attention" meditation app, based on the PAHM (Present Attention and Happiness Matrix) methodology founded by A.C. Amarasighe. Each stage builds upon the previous, creating a structured path for users to develop their meditation practice.

**Total Journey**: 6 Stages (115+ hours of practice)  
**Methodology**: PAHM (Present Attention and Happiness Matrix)  
**Progression**: Linear unlock system with minimum completion requirements  
**Session Types**: 
- **Stage 1**: Timer-only sessions (building stillness foundation)
- **Stage 2+**: Timer + PAHM Matrix sessions (attention tracking practice)

**Duration Rules**:
- **Meditation Sessions**: Minimum duration specified per stage (user can extend via slider)
- **Mind Recovery Exercises**: Fixed durations (non-adjustable):
  - Morning Recharge: 5 minutes
  - Mid-Day Reset: 3 minutes
  - Emotional Reset: 5 minutes
  - Work-Home Transition: 5 minutes
  - Bedtime Wind Down: 8 minutes

## **STAGE 2: PAHM TRAINEE**
*Introduction to Present Attention and Happiness Matrix*

### **Overview**
The PAHM Trainee stage introduces users to the revolutionary PAHM Matrix system, founded by A.C. Amarasighe. Users begin tracking their attention patterns across the 3×3 grid while maintaining physical stillness, learning to observe where their mind naturally goes without judgment.

**Session Interface**: 30-minute timer + Interactive 3×3 PAHM Matrix  
**Goal**: Develop awareness of attention patterns, not control them  
**Philosophy**: Happiness that stays through acceptance and observation

### **Understanding the PAHM Matrix**
The PAHM Matrix is a 3×3 grid mapping attention across two dimensions:

**Time Orientation**: Past, Present, Future  
**Emotional Charge**: Likes (Attachment), Neutral, Dislikes (Aversion)

```
┌─────────────┬─────────────┬─────────────┐
│  REGRET     │   PAST      │  NOSTALGIA  │
│ (Dislikes)  │ (Neutral)   │   (Likes)   │
├─────────────┼─────────────┼─────────────┤
│  DISLIKES   │  PRESENT    │    LIKES    │
│ (Aversion)  │  (Center)   │(Attachment) │
├─────────────┼─────────────┼─────────────┤
│   WORRY     │   FUTURE    │ANTICIPATION │
│ (Dislikes)  │ (Neutral)   │   (Likes)   │
└─────────────┴─────────────┴─────────────┘
```

### **The Nine Positions Explained**

#### **Present Moment (Center)**
Pure awareness in the here and now. This is mindfulness where attention is anchored in the present moment without judgment.

#### **Neutral Time Awareness**
- **Past (Neutral)**: Factual recall and learning from experience without emotional charge
- **Future (Neutral)**: Practical planning and preparation without anxiety or excessive hope

#### **Likes/Attachment Positions**
- **Nostalgia**: Attachment to pleasant memories, romanticizing past experiences
- **Likes**: Desire, craving, or clinging to current experiences
- **Anticipation**: Attachment to future possibilities, excitement about what might happen

#### **Dislikes/Aversion Positions**
- **Regret**: Aversion to past experiences, self-criticism, wishing things were different
- **Dislikes**: Resistance, avoidance, or rejection of current experiences
- **Worry**: Aversion to future possibilities, anxiety, fear, catastrophizing

### **Practice Details**
- **Minimum Duration**: 30 minutes per session (user-adjustable via duration slider)

### **How to Practice with PAHM Matrix**
1. **Maintain Physical Stillness**: Sit comfortably without moving
2. **Track Your Attention**: Whenever you notice where your attention has gone, tap the corresponding matrix position
3. **Observe Without Judgment**: Don't try to control or change where your mind goes
4. **Notice Patterns**: Simply observe which positions your attention gravitates toward
5. **Accept All Experiences**: The goal is awareness, not forcing attention to any particular position
